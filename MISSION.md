# Mission

## Why
Use Obsidian as the **viewer and navigator** of LLM-wiki style knowledge bases
([Karpathy pattern](https://gist.github.com/karpathy)): Claude writes and maintains the
Markdown pages, I read, filter and explore them.

Two real projects:

1. **Kolnatun BL wiki** (work, `C:\_repos\Azure\kol-natun-vb\Main\claude\kolnatun-context-2.0`):
   business logic extracted from a legacy VB6 app. I want to see links between calculators,
   shared / duplicate blocks, the source parts each result depends on, and later dead code.
2. **Personal media wiki** (home computer): films, books, lectures, with my comments and where
   each item is stored.

## What success looks like
- I can open any vault and in under a minute narrow the graph / search to exactly the layer
  I care about (results, sources, process).
- I can read and tweak Dataview / Bases queries that answer "what is shared", "what is
  duplicated", "what is not covered yet".
- I can tell Claude precisely which properties and links to write, because I know what
  Obsidian can do with them.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Writing notes by hand at scale (Claude does that).
- Obsidian Publish, themes, mobile.
